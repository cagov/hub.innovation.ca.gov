This chapter explains the policies, legal requirements, and procedures for state contracting. 

## Who this policy applies to:

* Certified Small Businesses (SB) 
* Microbusinesses (Micro) 
* Disabled Veteran Business Enterprises (DVBE) 
* Nonprofit Veteran Service Agencies (NVSA)

## What purchases this policy applies to:

* Non-IT goods and services
* Public works
* IT goods and services
* Telecommunications

When this chapter refers to a certified SB, it includes certified MBs. The Government Code defines who qualifies as a Microbusiness.

## Legal Requirements - What departments must do

**Small Business Procurement and Contract Act (Government Code Sections 14835–14843)**

**Military and Veterans Code (Section 999 et seq.)**

Whenever possible, state departments must provide contracting opportunities to California Certified SBs, MBs, and DVBEs.

### Assembly Bill 1574 (2021)

Work to increase participation by certified SBs, Micros, and DVBEs. Regularly look for certified businesses not worked with before or use often. Include these businesses when requesting quotes, bids, or offers.

### AB 1574 — previous contractors

Departments can continue to work with SBs, Micros, and DVBEs they have used before. Previous contracting with the state does not exclude businesses from future opportunities.

### Assembly Bill 2019 (2022)

Beginning January 1, 2023:

Each department must have an annual SB participation goal of at least 25%. This goal includes SBs and Micros. The goal applies to the department’s annual procurement and contracting activity.

## Procedure

Chapter 22, section 2200.02 outlines goals requirements. These goals help increase competition and give businesses access to state contracting opportunities.

Annual state procurement and contracting participation goals departments must meet:

* At least 25% for Small Business (SB) 
* At least 3% for Disabled Veteran Business Enterprise (DVBE)
 
If a department does not meet the annual goal they must:

* Submit an improvement plan to DGS by 1 October annually with their annual report.

If a department fails to meet SB or DVBE goals in three out of five years, DGS may take corrective action. Possible corrective actions include:

* Removing the department's delegated contracting authority
* Imposing restrictions on contracting activities

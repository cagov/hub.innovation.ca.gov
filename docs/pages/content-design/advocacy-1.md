## INTRODUCTION

### POLICY

This chapter explains the policies, legal requirements, and procedures for including certified Small Businesses (SB), Microbusinesses (Micro), Disabled Veteran Business Enterprises (DVBE), and Nonprofit Veteran Service Agencies (NVSA) in state contracting.

Under the Small Business Procurement and Contract Act (Government Code Sections 14835–14843) and the Military and Veterans Code (Section 999 et seq.), all state departments must provide contracting opportunities to California-certified SBs, Micros, and DVBEs whenever possible.

Unless otherwise stated, any reference to a certified SB in this chapter also includes certified Microbusinesses as defined in the Government Code.

Under CA Assembly Bill 1574 (est. 2021), each department must make ongoing efforts to increase the number of California-certified SBs, Micros, and DVBEs participating in state contracts. Departments should regularly identify certified firms that have not previously done business with the department or are not frequently used, and include them when requesting quotes, bids, or offers.

This requirement does not exclude SBs, Micros, or DVBEs that have previously contracted with the state from future opportunities.

Since January 1, 2023, Assembly Bill 2019 (est. 2022) builds upon AB 1574 by introducing stronger accountability and equity measures. It codifies a 25% small‑business participation goal, requiring every state department to direct at least 25% of its contracting dollars to SBs, including Micros, DVBEs, and other disadvantaged small businesses.

This policy applies to all purchases, including:

* Non-IT goods and services
* Public works
* IT goods and services
* Telecommunications

### PROCEDURE

Each department must meet annual statewide participation goals in state procurement and contracting:

* At least 25% for Small Business (SB) participation
* At least 3% for Disabled Veteran Business Enterprise (DVBE) participation

These goals are designed to promote competition and ensure all businesses have fair access to state contracting opportunities.

Departments that do not meet these goals must submit an Improvement Plan to the Department of General Services (DGS) by October 1 each year, as outlined in Chapter 22, Section 2200.2, along with their annual report.

If a department fails to meet its SB or DVBE goals in three out of five years, DGS may take corrective actions, which can include:

* Removing the department’s delegated contracting authority
* Imposing additional restrictions on contracting activities

The consolidated annual report will include any enforcement actions taken.

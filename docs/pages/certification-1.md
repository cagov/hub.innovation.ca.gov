Dear Jamie Lee,

The Office of Small Business and Disabled Veteran Business Enterprise Services (OSDS) completed a review of your appeal documentation and original certification application, including the denial of your certification. Small Business certification is based upon an applicant's ability to request, meet, and maintain eligibility requirements.  

Regarding JL Incorporated, OSDS’ certification decision is final, and your request for an appeal is hereby rejected. Unfortunately, your request for an appeal is not based on acceptable grounds according to the California Code of Regulations, Title 2, Section 1896.22, subsections (c) and (f)(3). 

The Small Business certification denial is based on JL Incorporated’s inability to meet all eligibility requirements. These are found in California Code of Regulations, Title 2, Section 1896.22(c) and (f)(3)

The business did not provide sufficient documentation to verify that it meets the ownership requirements for Small Business certification. Our findings and final decision are based on the information contained in the application and any supplemental material submitted to OSDS upon its request. The supporting documents include the following:  
 
* Original Small Business certification application
* Supplemental ownership documents submitted during the certification review

Please note, this determination does not prevent your company from bidding on State of California contracts or re-applying for certification once your firm meets the eligibility requirements.

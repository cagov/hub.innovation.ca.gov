<div class="styleguide">

The ODI Data Visualization Style Guide gives our team a shared foundation for creating data visualizations. It aims to simplify design decisions and provide a consistent look and feel. It also helps our team create visualizations that are easy to understand.

The guide covers common chart design patterns, including color, typography, and visual styling. It may not address every use case, but it gives a starting point for making thoughtful design choices.

This is a living guide that will evolve as our team's needs change.

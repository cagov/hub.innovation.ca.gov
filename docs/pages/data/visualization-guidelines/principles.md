## Clarity

People need to easily understand data. Cluttered, ambiguous, or poorly structured visualizations are hard to interpret. Clear visualizations let people understand insights immediately.

## Accuracy

People rely on data to make decisions. Inaccurate and misleading data can lead to misunderstandings, poor decisions, and loss of trust.

## Accessibility

People with a wide range of abilities and contexts use our data visualizations. Design with accessibility in mind from the beginning. Accessibility improves comprehension for everyone.

## Equity

Data visualizations influence how people understand communities and outcomes. Thoughtful design helps avoid harm, bias or exclusion. It also makes data easier to understand and use, including by the communities represented in the data.

## Human-centered

Data visualizations should serve people’s needs. Design with the audience in mind. This gives people data that is relevant, usable, and actionable.

## Transparency

Transparency builds trust. It helps people understand the context and limits of the data.

<div class="styleguide">

Leave the same room around and inside every ODI chart, so charts line up on a page and read the same way.

<img src="/img/dataviz/styleguide/spacing-redline.png" width="876" height="459" style="max-width: 100%; height: auto;" alt="Spacing diagram of a bar chart titled One measure, one color, showing average daily PM2.5 by county in 2020 for Contra Costa 11.1, Alameda 11.0, San Francisco 10.5, San Mateo 9.8 and Marin 8.0. Pink bands and tags mark the spacing: 24 pixels top padding, 8 from title to subtitle, 24 from subtitle to chart, 40 side padding on each side, 8 from category label to bar, 8 from bar to value, 6-pixel tick marks, 8 from tick to axis number, 16 from axis numbers to source, and 16 bottom padding.">

**With a legend:** the legend sits `24px` below the subtitle and `16px` above the chart. Each color swatch is `8px` from its label, with `16px` between items.

<img src="/img/dataviz/styleguide/spacing-redline-legend.png" width="876" height="507" style="max-width: 100%; height: auto;" alt="Spacing diagram of a 100% stacked bar chart titled Most workers drive, except in San Francisco, showing how workers 16 and over get to work by county in 2024. A legend above the chart names four parts: car, truck, or van; public transit; worked from home; and other. Car shares run from 74% in Contra Costa and 71% in San Mateo to 66% in Alameda, 61% in Marin and 36% in San Francisco, where transit is 25%. Pink tags mark 24 pixels from subtitle to legend, 16 from legend to chart, 8 from swatch to label, 16 between legend items, plus the same padding, tick and source spacing as the chart above. Source: U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 2024 1-year estimates, table B08301.">

- **Top padding:** `24px` (`16px` on mobile)
- **Side padding, left and right:** `40px` (`16px` on mobile)
- **Title to subtitle:** `8px`
- **Subtitle to chart, or to the legend:** `24px`
- **Legend to chart:** `16px`
- **Category label to bar:** `8px`
- **Bar to its value:** `8px`
- **Bars:** any thickness that fits the chart, with the gap between bars narrower than one bar
- **Tick marks:** `6px`, hanging down from the bottom of the chart
- **Tick to axis number:** `8px`
- **Axis numbers to source:** `16px`
- **Bottom padding:** `16px`

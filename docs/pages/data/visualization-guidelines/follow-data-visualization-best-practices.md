Learn how to create visualizations that are clear, accurate, accessible, and easy to use. These best practices apply across chart types.

**Best practices for specific chart types:** Visit [Choose the right chart](data/visualization-guidelines/choose-the-right-chart) for best practices for individual chart types, such as bar charts, line charts, and scatter plots.

## Explore best practices:

### [Writing effective text](/data/visualization-guidelines/follow-data-visualization-best-practices/writing-effective-text)

Write titles, labels, and annotations that help people understand the data.

### [Color and contrast](/data/visualization-guidelines/follow-data-visualization-best-practices/color-and-contrast)

Use color to communicate information clearly.

### [Equity](/data/visualization-guidelines/follow-data-visualization-best-practices/equity)

Consider how visualizations may affect different communities.

### [Accessibility](/data/visualization-guidelines/follow-data-visualization-best-practices/accessibility)

Make visualizations easy to use for as many people.

### [Transparency](/data/visualization-guidelines/follow-data-visualization-best-practices/transparency)

Ensure that people understand and trust your data.

### [Usability](/data/visualization-guidelines/follow-data-visualization-best-practices/usability)

Make visualizations easy to interact with.

### [Density and simplicity](/data/visualization-guidelines/follow-data-visualization-best-practices/density-and-simplicity)

Find the right balance between including what’s important and removing what’s not.

### [Chart integrity](/data/visualization-guidelines/follow-data-visualization-best-practices/chart-integrity)

Represent data accurately and avoid misleading visualizations.

Thank you all for meeting to talk about data sharing between our departments.

Who needs to attend:

* Data Recipient: Program contacts responsible for the business use case
* Data Provider: Program contacts with a deep understanding of the data to exchange. They will inform and advise on the business use case needs.

What to bring to the meeting:

* Data Recipient: Draft business use case proposal
* Data Provider: A list of available data fields

Meeting objectives:

* Refine business use case needs
* Develop preliminary list of data fields

Agenda:

1. Introductions (all parties)
2. Why it’s important to share this data and how we’ll use it (Data Recipient)
3. Discuss data needs in support of use case (all parties)
  * List and refine data fields to meet the business use case
  * Flag issues that need input from legal or security. We won’t resolve them at this meeting
4. Identify next steps. At least, cover how to:
  * Finalize list of data elements in scope
  * Get input from legal and security

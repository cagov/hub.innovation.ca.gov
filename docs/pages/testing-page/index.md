## Step list

Numbered steps joined by a vertical line, as on [innovation.ca.gov](https://innovation.ca.gov/join-us/applying-civil-service-job/#how-to-apply). Write a markdown numbered list where each item starts with a `###` heading, and wrap it in a `cagov-step-list` div. Leave a blank line after the opening `<div>` and before the closing `</div>`, or the list won't be parsed as markdown.

```markdown
<div class="cagov-step-list">

1. ### Create or sign in to your CalCareers account

   If you don't already have one...

2. ### Find the job posting you want to apply to

   You can find all ODI jobs...

</div>
```

## How to apply

<div class="cagov-step-list">

1. ### Create or sign in to your CalCareers account

   If you don’t already have one, it’s [easy to make one](https://www.calcareers.ca.gov/CalHRPublic/CreateNewAccount.aspx). Work for California has a video and guide for creating a CalCareers account in their [job seeker resources.](https://workforcalifornia.ca.gov/resources)

2. ### Find the job posting you want to apply to

   You can [find all ODI jobs](https://jobs.ca.gov/CalHRPublic/Search/JobSearchResults.aspx#depid=312&appmethid=1) by selecting **Office of Data and Innovation** in the Department filter.

3. ### Prepare your documents

   Start by confirming you meet the **Minimum Requirements**. Get any transcripts you need.

   We recommend writing your **Statement of Qualifications** (SOQ) by first: including the question, then second: writing your response underneath it in a narrative format. We score the SOQ using a rubric and invite the top-scoring candidates to interview.

   For each question, include details about:

   - Years of experience
   - Relevant roles
   - Relevant desirable qualifications listed in the job announcement
   - 2-3 examples (unless stated otherwise)

   Your SOQ is 2 pages (unless stated otherwise). Use single spacing and 12-point, Arial font. Work for California has a guide on how to write an SOQ in their [job seeker resources.](https://workforcalifornia.ca.gov/resources)

4. ### Select Apply now

   You can find the button in the top right of the job posting.

5. ### Select I have eligibility

   ![The Can I apply for this job section of the job application. The option "I have eligibility" is circled.](https://live-digital-ca-gov.pantheonsite.io/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/can-i-apply-for-this-job.png)

   Choose this option even if you have not yet taken the exam for the job.

6. ### Complete the application

   If you’ve applied to a state job before, you can use a previous template. Select *Use Application Template*.

   If this is your first time applying to a state job, select *Use Blank Application*.

   Be detailed when listing your duties in your current and past positions. This helps the application review panel fully assess your experience and abilities.

   Complete all 4 steps listed on the left side of the page. Fill out all required fields and upload your documents. Work for California has an application checklist in their [job seeker resources.](https://workforcalifornia.ca.gov/resources)

7. ### Submit your application

   When you’re done, select the **Save & Review Application** button on the bottom right.

   On the next screen, review your application. If everything looks right, scroll down to the **Submit Application** section. Choose how you want to submit your application.

   - Electronically: check the 2 application certification boxes. Select the **Submit my Application Electronically** button. You will receive an Application Receipt.
   - By mail or drop off: follow the instructions.

</div>

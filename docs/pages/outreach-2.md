Sign up to provide goods and services to the state during an emergency

The state uses the **SB/DVBE Emergency Registry** to find suppliers during an emergency. The Registry lists certified businesses that can provide goods and services for emergencies. This portal is different from Cal eProcure.

## Eligibility

Your business qualifies for the emergency registry if it:

* Is SB/DVBE certified
* Offers one or more goods or services in the categories listed below

Emergencies require fast, around-the-clock turnaround times. Only sign up if you can immediately provide the goods and services. You must be ready, willing, and able.

## Before You Sign Up

Have these ready:

* SB/DVBE Certification ID number
* Emergency categories
* Description of products/services
* Location of business
* Business contact information, including an emergency contact that can be reached around-the-clock
  
Follow [step-by-step instructions](https://www.dgs.ca.gov/-/media/Divisions/PD/OSDS/Outreach/Resources/CertifiedBusiness_Instructions.pdf) to sign up.
